Support

Questions or problems? Write to littlechats [at] amauryvela.com and we'll answer as soon as we can.

Do my chats leave my iPhone?

No. They stay on your device and in your iCloud. You can share with people you invite.

Why does the app download a model?

Transcription runs on your iPhone. The one-time download is about 630 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.

I don't see summaries.

Summaries need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Chats work without them.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data.
