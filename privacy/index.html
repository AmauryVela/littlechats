Privacy

The short version

We collect nothing. Little Chats has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you record ever reaches us.

Where your chats live

Audio, transcripts and summaries stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Only people you invite can see them, and only if you choose to share.

Models and summaries

The first time you transcribe, the app downloads its speech models (Whisper and SpeakerKit) from Hugging Face. Hugging Face can see your device's IP address, as any website does. Summaries are written on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Children's voices

Little Chats is made for parents. Your child's voice is stored only on your devices and in your iCloud, and nothing reaches us.

Delete everything

Open Settings → Delete all my data in the app. It removes your chats from this iPhone and from your iCloud.
